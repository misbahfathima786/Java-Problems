## 📌 Number Properties Covered

### Friendly Pair

Two numbers are considered a Friendly Pair when the ratio of the sum of their proper divisors to the number is the same.

### Deficient Number

A number is **Deficient** when the sum of its proper divisors is less than the number.

### Abundant Number

A number is **Abundant** when the sum of its proper divisors is greater than the number.

### Harshad Number

A number is a **Harshad Number** when it is divisible by the sum of its digits.

### Automorphic Number

A number is **Automorphic** when its square ends with the same digits as the original number.

### Perfect Square

A number is a **Perfect Square** if it is the square of an integer.

### Perfect Number

A number is **Perfect** when the sum of its proper divisors is equal to the number.

### Strong Number

A number is **Strong** when the sum of the factorials of its digits is equal to the original number.

### Armstrong Number

A number is an **Armstrong Number** when the sum of each digit raised to the power of the number of digits is equal to the original number.
